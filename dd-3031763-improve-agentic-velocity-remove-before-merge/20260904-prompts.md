The readability problems are therefore structural:

1. The driver does not announce most phase transitions. It updates `$currentPhase`, but ordinarily prints nothing. Its `[shepherd-control]` prefix is currently used only for post-mortem recovery.
2. Three different layers use `[shepherd-task]`. The generated stage-20 launcher, stage-25 orchestrator, and per-issue worker look like one emitter.

4. Full multiline prompts dominate the stream. They are useful evidence, but obscure campaign progress.
5. The transient `Session exported to:` paths look durable. In reality those temporary files are moved and the directories deleted.
6. Stage 15 prints a manual handoff command during an automated run. That makes it look as though user action is required.
7. Color is carrying information that prefixes and structure should carry. Once color is stripped, layer boundaries disappear.
8. Child processes write through unchanged. `Invoke-CheckedPwshScript` provides exit-code checking but no labeling, indentation, or start/end boundaries.
