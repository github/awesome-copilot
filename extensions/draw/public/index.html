N and Shift+N select the next or previous element. Enter edits its label, the arrow keys move it and Delete removes it. To add a shape, press R for a rectangle, O for an ellipse, D for a diamond or C for a database, then press Enter. T and then Enter adds text. To connect two shapes, select one, press A and then Enter, select the other with N, and press Enter. Tab moves on to the style bar and the tools. Press question mark for all shortcuts.

Start a diagram

Pick a shape on the left (or press R), then click the canvas or press Enter.

Hover a shape and drag a + handle to connect it, or click + to add the next step.
