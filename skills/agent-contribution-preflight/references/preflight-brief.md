# Owner preflight brief

Use this structure before an external contribution to a high-attention or unknown repository.

## Target

- Repository and proposed external action
- Why this contribution belongs in the project
- Exact owner approval still required

## Current rules

- Contribution policy
- AI or automation policy
- Issue/PR template requirements
- Visible automated-contributor controls and settings
- Unknown or unobservable controls

## Community precedent

- One to three relevant Issue, PR, or Discussion URLs
- What maintainers objected to
- Whether the objection concerned policy, review burden, evidence, communication, provenance, or volume
- What has changed in the current plan so the failure is not repeated

## Evidence packet

- Minimal reproduction or motivating evidence
- Diff scope
- Exact tests and results
- Limitations and unresolved questions
- AI assistance and human verification

## Account-interest recommendation

- Likely repository-local consequence
- Community-list or reputation risk, if any
- Recommended choice: proceed, ask maintainers first, keep working locally, use an approved identified bot, or decline
- Why that choice protects both the owner's account and maintainer time
- Local assistance that will continue even if the external write is frozen
- Which parts, if any, the owner must author, run, verify, or communicate personally
