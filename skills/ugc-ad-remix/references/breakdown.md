# Breaking down a UGC ad

A UGC ad works because of its structure, not its exact words. Break it into parts you can rebuild with a different product.

## The hook

The hook is the first line or first shot, usually the first two or three seconds. Quote it exactly, then classify it as one of six types:

| Type | What it does | Example shape |
|---|---|---|
| problem | Names a pain the viewer already feels | "My coffee is always cold by 9am." |
| curiosity | Opens a gap the viewer wants closed | "Nobody told me this about travel mugs." |
| social-proof | Leans on other people choosing it | "The mug every nurse on my floor bought." |
| pov | Puts the viewer inside a moment | "POV: you finally found a bag that fits your laptop." |
| before-after | Shows a change | "Week one vs week four." |
| negativity | Warns, complains or rejects | "Stop buying cheap mugs." |

Add one sentence on why it works for this audience. If the hook is visual only (no words), describe the shot.

## Segments

Split the ad into ordered segments and give each one role:

- **hook**: stops the scroll.
- **problem**: makes the pain concrete.
- **demo**: shows the product being used.
- **proof**: evidence the viewer can see or a result shown on camera.
- **offer**: price, discount, bundle or guarantee.
- **cta**: what to do next.

Not every ad has every role, and roles can repeat. Keep the real order; that order is what you will reuse.

## Selling points

List what the ad actually claims, at most five, in the ad's own terms. These are the competitor's marketing claims. Record them so you know what the ad relies on, not as facts.

## Call to action

Quote the closing line and name its type: "shop now", "link in bio", "comment for the link", "discount code", or none.

## What transfers

Write two or three moves another brand could reuse, as instructions. Good ones are about structure and delivery:

- "Admit you were skeptical before showing the result."
- "Name the exact moment the problem happens."
- "Show the product failing a test the old product failed, then passing it."

Not transferable: the competitor's price, discount, statistics, brand name, or claims about their own product.

## When there is no transcript

If you only have a link and cannot get the words, ask the user to paste the caption and what is said. Do not reconstruct an ad from its URL, its thumbnail, or memory.
