# Dating photo checklist

Use this when the DatePhotos.AI connector is not available, or to explain a score it returned. Score each photo 0–100 by starting at 100 and subtracting for problems. Say clearly that scores without the connector are your own judgement.

## Disqualifiers for the lead photo (do not use as photo 1)

- More than one person, or it is unclear which person is the user
- Face hidden or very small: sunglasses, hat brim shadow, mask, back to camera, face under ~10% of the frame
- Heavily filtered, beauty-smoothed, or AI-altered so that the user no longer looks like themselves
- Very old photo — the user looks noticeably different now
- Contains a QR code, URL, handle or contact details (several apps prohibit this)

## Deductions

| Problem | Typical deduction |
|---|---|
| Dark, harsh or mixed lighting on the face | −10 to −20 |
| Blurry, low resolution, heavy compression | −10 to −25 |
| Cluttered or distracting background (messy room, bathroom, car interior) | −5 to −15 |
| Bathroom mirror selfie or obvious arm-length selfie | −10 |
| Cropped-out ex / other person partly visible | −15 |
| Forced or no expression, eyes closed | −5 to −10 |
| Unflattering angle (from below, extreme close-up) | −5 to −10 |
| Same outfit or setting repeated across many photos | −5 per repeat |

## What a strong lineup looks like (4–6 photos)

1. **Lead:** clear head-and-shoulders, good natural light, genuine smile or relaxed expression, simple background
2. **Full body:** shows the user's whole look honestly
3. **Activity or hobby:** something the user actually does, ideally a conversation starter
4. **Social:** with friends, user easy to identify (never as photo 1)
5. **Travel or setting:** adds context, still recognisable
6. **Recent unedited photo:** builds trust and helps verification

## Verification risk

Apps with photo verification compare a live selfie or video selfie against profile photos. Flag any photo that may cause a mismatch: heavy edits, AI-generated photos, very old photos, drastic hairstyle or weight changes, or photos where the face is barely visible.
