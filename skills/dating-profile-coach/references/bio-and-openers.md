# Bio, prompts and openers

## Bio rules

- Use only facts the user gave you. Never invent a job, hobby, trip, achievement, height or trait.
- Keep the user's voice. If they are dry, stay dry; if they use emoji, keep a few.
- Length: check the app's current character limit in the bio field itself (limits change). Hinge relies on prompt answers more than a free-text bio. Short bios — two to four lines — read best on every app.
- One specific detail beats three adjectives. "Makes a decent ramen from scratch" beats "foodie, adventurous, fun".
- End with something easy to reply to: a question, a mild opinion, or a choice ("best taco spot in town — fight me").
- Cut clichés: "love to laugh", "partner in crime", "fluent in sarcasm", "just ask", "here for a good time", lists of zodiac signs or height requirements unless the user insists.
- Say what they are looking for in one plain line if the user wants that clear.

## Prompt answers (Hinge-style)

Answer with a specific story or opinion, not a list. A good answer gives a match an obvious follow-up question. Avoid answers that are only a joke with nothing to reply to.

## Openers

Write three, each tied to something specific in the match's photos or prompts. Format: one observation + one easy question. Avoid "hey", compliments on looks, pickup lines, and anything sexual. Keep each under 25 words.

Example structure (adapt, do not copy):
- "That trail in your third photo looks steep — was the view worth it, or is that face pure regret?"
- "You said you'll judge me by my coffee order. Oat flat white: pass or fail?"
