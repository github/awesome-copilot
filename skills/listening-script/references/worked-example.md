# Worked example: a report that must remain an experiment

This is a fictional training fixture, not a product benchmark or user testimony.

## Source

In a preliminary internal test on twelve devices, version A had a median wait of ten seconds and version B had a median wait of eight seconds. Each device ran the same passage once per version. Three devices were faster with A. Battery use was not measured. No users were surveyed, and no statistical confidence interval was calculated.

| Version | Median wait | Devices tested |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | 10 seconds | 12 |
| B | 8 seconds | 12 |

## Request

Turn the paragraph and table into a short spoken explanation in Chinese. Keep the evidence limits. Do not add product recommendations.

## Narration

这是一轮初步的内部测试，还不能代表所有设备上的体验。测试人员在十二台设备上，用两个版本各运行了一遍同一段文字。

版本A的等待时间中位数是十秒，版本B是八秒。也就是说，在这轮测试里，B的中位数少了两秒，相比A缩短了百分之二十。不过，十二台设备中仍有三台是A更快，所以不能说B在每台设备上都更快。

这次没有测量电池消耗，也没有调查真实用户，更没有计算统计置信区间。因此，我们只能描述这一小轮测试里的等待时间差异，不能据此断言B更省电，或者用户一定更喜欢B。

## Source and editorial notes

- Source: the fictional fixture above; no external verification claimed.
- Both versions used twelve devices, with one run per device per version.
- Ten to eight seconds is a twenty-percent reduction relative to A. It is not a twenty-percentage-point change.
- Three devices favoring A, absent battery measurements, absent user surveys, and absent confidence intervals remain audible.
- The table became sentences; no data was silently removed. There is no product mention in the script.

For English output, retain the same claims and limits rather than simply making the prose more emphatic.
