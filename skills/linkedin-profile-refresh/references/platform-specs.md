# Platform photo specs (official, reviewed 2026-10-14)

Only values from each platform's own help pages. "Not published" means the platform does not state it — do not fill it in from blogs.

| Platform | Profile photo | Cover / banner | File | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn (personal) | 400×400 to 7680×4320 px | 1584×396 px recommended | max 8 MB; PNG or JPG; no GIF | [Photo won't upload](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a549049), [Cover image](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a568217) |
| LinkedIn Page (company) | logo min 268×268, recommended 400×400 | 1512×256 px | max 3 MB; PNG or JPEG | [Page image specs](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a563309) |
| Slack | min 512×512, max 1024×1024 px | — | not published | [Upload a profile photo](https://slack.com/help/articles/115005506003-Upload-a-profile-photo) |
| GitHub | not published (crop after upload; square works best) | — | not published | [Personalize your profile](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/tutorials/personalize-your-profile) |

LinkedIn content rule: "You can use an illustration, caricature, or other artistic rendering of yourself, but your profile photo must reflect your likeness." ([Profile photo guidelines](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1377087))

GitHub bio limit: 160 characters.
