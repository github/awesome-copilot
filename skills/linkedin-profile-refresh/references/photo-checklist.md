# Professional photo checklist

Use this when the ProfessionalHeadshot.io connector is not available, or to explain a score it returned. Start at 100 and subtract. Say clearly that scores without the connector are your own judgement.

## Disqualifiers for a LinkedIn or resume photo

- More than one person, or a cropped-out partner still visible
- Face hidden: sunglasses, hat brim shadow, mask, back to camera
- Not a likeness of the user (LinkedIn removes logos, landscapes, animals, other people, childhood photos)
- Heavily filtered or AI-altered so the user no longer looks like themselves
- Very old photo — the user looks noticeably different now

## Deductions

| Problem | Typical deduction |
|---|---|
| Face too small for a circular thumbnail (face under ~40% of the frame height) | −10 to −20 |
| Harsh, dark or mixed-colour lighting on the face | −10 to −20 |
| Blurry, low resolution (short edge under 400 px), heavy compression | −10 to −25 |
| Busy or casual background (party, car interior, bathroom) | −5 to −15 |
| Arm-length selfie angle or from below | −5 to −10 |
| Outfit clearly off for the target industry | −5 to −10 |
| Forced or no expression | −5 |

## What a strong professional headshot looks like

- Head and top of shoulders, eyes roughly on the upper third line
- Soft, even light from the front or side (a window works)
- Plain or softly blurred background with contrast against hair and clothes
- Attire one step more formal than the user's everyday work clothes
- Relaxed, genuine expression; eyes to camera
