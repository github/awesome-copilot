# Content Realism

Use this when generating copy, examples, mock data, names, metrics, testimonials, product labels, empty states, errors, or placeholders.

## Principle

Mock content should feel like draft production content, not filler. Users judge quality through words and data as much as layout.

## Names And Brands

Avoid:

- John Doe, Jane Smith, Sarah Chan.
- Acme, Globex, Nexus, SmartFlow, Nova, Apex when they are contextless.
- Generic team names like Design Team, Product Team, Marketing Team when specificity would help.

Use:

- Names that fit the product's locale, market, and audience.
- Brand names with a concrete category or metaphor.
- Team names tied to actual workflows: Revenue Ops, Claims Review, Studio Booking, Vendor Risk.

## Metrics And Data

Avoid:

- `99.99%`, `50%`, `100%`, `$100.00`, `1234567`.
- Identical dates across lists.
- Perfectly round chart values.
- Tables where every row is the same length and shape.

Use:

- Organic but plausible values: `47.2%`, `18.6 days`, `$98.40`, `1,284`, `+12.7%`.
- Mixed dates, durations, and statuses.
- Numbers that support the story of the interface.
- Tabular figures for data-heavy UI.

## Copy

Avoid:

- Elevate, Seamless, Unleash, Next-Gen, Game-changer, Delve, Tapestry, Revolutionary, Cutting-edge.
- "Oops!" for serious errors.
- Exclamation marks in routine success messages.
- Placeholder Latin text.
- Button labels like Submit, OK, Click Here, Learn More when a clearer verb exists.

Use:

- Concrete verbs: Create, Save changes, Invite vendor, Review claim, Export CSV, Start check.
- Direct errors: "Connection failed. Try again." or "Password must be at least 8 characters."
- Helpful empty states: what belongs here, why it is empty, and the one next action.
- Sentence case by default unless the design system specifies otherwise.

## Avatar And Media Placeholders

Avoid:

- Reusing the same avatar for multiple people.
- Generic user icons for people-heavy products.
- Unreliable image URLs.
- Stock-looking imagery when the product requires trust or inspection.

Use:

- Unique seeds per person or object.
- Realistic initials or generated avatars when photos are not appropriate.
- Product-specific placeholder imagery.
- Consistent art direction across all media.

## Empty States

An empty state should answer:

- What will appear here?
- Why is it empty right now?
- What can the user do next?

Keep it short. One primary action is usually enough.

## Error And Success Tone

- Be calm and specific.
- Explain recovery.
- Do not blame the user.
- Do not over-celebrate routine actions.
- Put the message near the relevant control when possible.
