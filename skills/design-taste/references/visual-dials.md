# Visual Dials

Use these dials to calibrate visual direction without turning taste into a pile of absolute rules.

## The Three Dials

### Design Variance

How conventional or surprising the composition should be.

- **1-3: Predictable.** Symmetrical, centered, familiar, low-risk. Good for admin, settings, healthcare, finance, compliance, internal tools.
- **4-7: Distinctive.** Asymmetry, varied section rhythm, intentional offsets, stronger type contrast. Good for polished SaaS, product pages, portfolios, editorial product UI.
- **8-10: Art-directed.** Broken grids, large whitespace, unusual image crops, expressive pacing. Good for campaigns, launch pages, creative portfolios, brand moments.

Guardrail: high variance must still collapse cleanly on mobile. At small widths, prioritize order, readability, and touch targets over preserving desktop drama.

### Motion Intensity

How much animation the interface can carry.

- **1-3: Quiet.** Hover, focus, active states only. Good for dense tools, repeated workflows, dashboards, settings, data entry.
- **4-7: Fluid.** Page or section reveals, tab indicators, popovers, toasts, subtle shared transitions. Good for most product UI.
- **8-10: Choreographed.** Scrolltelling, pinned sections, 3D/canvas, complex timelines, cinematic reveals. Good for marketing, storytelling, product launches, demos.

Guardrail: motion intensity should drop as task frequency rises. The more often users perform an action, the less animation it should have.

### Visual Density

How much information belongs on one screen.

- **1-3: Airy.** Premium, editorial, sparse, image-led, high whitespace.
- **4-7: Balanced.** Standard app/product density with clear grouping and scanability.
- **8-10: Dense.** Dashboards, command centers, data tools, operational workflows.

Guardrail: density does not mean cramming. Dense UI needs stronger alignment, smaller type hierarchy, tabular figures, dividers, and reduced decoration.

## Defaults By Product Type

- **Operational SaaS:** variance 3-5, motion 1-3, density 6-9.
- **Marketing landing page:** variance 5-8, motion 4-7, density 2-5.
- **Portfolio or creative studio:** variance 7-10, motion 5-8, density 1-4.
- **Financial/admin dashboard:** variance 2-4, motion 1-3, density 7-10.
- **Consumer mobile app:** variance 4-7, motion 4-7, density 4-7.
- **Luxury/editorial brand:** variance 5-8, motion 2-5, density 1-3.

## How To Apply

Before designing, infer the dial settings from the product, audience, and task. If the user gives direction, let it override the defaults.

Use the dials to decide:

- Whether to use symmetry or asymmetry.
- Whether cards should be framed or content should float.
- Whether motion should be invisible, fluid, or cinematic.
- Whether whitespace should expand or tighten.
- Whether typography should be utilitarian or expressive.

Do not expose the dials to the user unless it helps explain a design choice.
