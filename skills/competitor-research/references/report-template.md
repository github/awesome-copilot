# Competitor brief structure

Adapt length to the task. Omit empty sections rather than padding the report.

1. **Finding and scope:** domain/product, research question, user's context, as-of date, public-web or supplied-materials mode.
2. **Product and business model:** audience, task, positioning, price/unit, free-to-paid boundary, sources and dates.
3. **Timeline:** event date, event, source, what it proves, uncertainty. Earliest observed activity is not founding.
4. **Channel evidence:** channel, observed asset/activity, traffic or conversion evidence if available, what remains unknown.
5. **Experiments:** up to three, each grounded in a cited observation, with hypothesis, prerequisites, small test, success measure and review/stop condition.
6. **Gaps:** unresolved claims and the smallest additional evidence needed. Explicitly distinguish unobserved from absent.

Keep citations near claims. Use clear labels for company claims and provider estimates. A short complete brief with honest gaps is better than an unsupported exhaustive report.
