# Guided interview protocol

## Stages
1. Learning goals and outcomes
2. Experience and prerequisites
3. Environment, time, subscriptions, budget and restrictions
4. Architecture and feasibility review
5. Curriculum proposal and learner approval

At each meaningful transition display **completed / in progress / pending**, with the actual decisions, blockers and next step. Do not mark a stage complete merely because a question was asked.

Ask only questions that materially affect scope, safety, cost, architecture or feasibility. If the learner supplies a full brief, extract it and skip redundant questions. Minor issues: recommend and proceed. Material issues: explain and ask one focused question. Unsafe or infeasible operations: do not execute; propose alternatives.

Record recommendations, learner decisions, overrides, assumptions and unresolved risks. Learners may override non-safety educational choices. Approval of a plan is distinct from approval of cloud spending, destructive changes, or overwrites.
