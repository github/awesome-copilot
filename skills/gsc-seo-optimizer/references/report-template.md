# Report and baseline

Keep the user-facing report concise; save detail when useful.

- Scope: property, source, search type, target market/device/page filters, current and comparison dates, as-of date, latest available finalized day, declared versus verified export scope.
- Trend: independently sourced clicks/impressions/CTR; explain brand contribution and query mix when supported. Otherwise say visible rows only.
- Opportunities: URL, metrics, actual supporting queries, business relevance, diagnosis, proposed action, confidence, review interval.
- Skips: recently changed, irrelevant, brand sitelinks, insufficient evidence, or no actionable issue.
- Limitations: row caps, missing query-page linkage, hidden queries, incomplete days, missing indexing evidence.
- Next batch: concrete pages and changes, or a justified no-action conclusion.

For an approved edit save a baseline JSON/Markdown artifact containing exact scope, metrics/source files, changed URLs, edit/deployment date, prior/new title or content summary, and planned review date. Reuse that same scope when reviewing. Do not commit private exports or upload them without authorization.
